# The Origin and Development of Psychoanalysis (1910)
**Author**: Sigmund Freud  
**Lectures**: Delivered at the Celebration of the 20th Anniversary of Clark University, 1909; published in American Journal of Psychology, 1910, 21, 181-218.  
**Translator**: Harry W. Chase (revised by Freud).

---

## Core Summary of the 5 Lectures

### First Lecture: The Origin & Cathartic Method (Breuer & Anna O.)
- Credit to Dr. Joseph Breuer (1880-1882) and the case of Anna O. (hysterical girl of 21). Symptoms: paralysis, anesthesia, eye-movement disturbance, tussis nervosa, hydrophobia (disgust of dog drinking from glass), alteration of personality/absences.
- "Talking cure" / "chimney sweeping": Recalling pathogenic situations under hypnosis and expressing pent-up affect led to permanent removal of symptoms.
- Formula: "Our hysterical patients suffer from reminiscences." Symptoms are memory symbols of traumatic experiences (psychic traumata), determined by the scene.
- Conversion: Pent-up emotion undergoes abnormal somatic innervation (hysterical conversion).

### Second Lecture: From Hypnosis to Free Association & Repression
- Influence of Charcot and Pierre Janet (congenital weakness of psychic synthesis).
- Freud rejected hypnosis due to inapplicability to all patients and masking of resistance.
- Bernheim's Nancy clinic insight: Forgotten somnambulic memories can be awakened in normal state with pressure/insistence.
- Resistance and Repression (Verdrängung): The force opposing recovery of memories is the very force that produced the forgetting. Conflict arises when a wish conflicts with ethical/aesthetic pretensions of the ego.

### Third Lecture: Free Association, Wit, and Dream Analysis
- Free association (Einfall) and the concept of "Complex" (Bleuler, Jung).
- Wit and jokes as disguised surrogates for repressed thoughts.
- Dream Interpretation (Die Traumdeutung) is the "via regia" (royal road) to the unconscious.
- Manifest dream-content vs. Latent dream-thoughts. Dream-work (Traumarbeit): condensation (Verdichtung) and displacement (Verschiebung). Dreams as disguised wish-fulfillments. Bungled actions (Fehlhandlungen).

### Fourth Lecture: Infantile Sexuality & Etiology of Neuroses
- Symptoms trace back regularly to impressions from erotic/sexual sphere (Triebkomponente).
- Neuroses arise from childhood impressions. Auto-erotism (Havelock Ellis), erogenous zones (thumb-sucking as sexual satisfaction).
- Oedipus complex (King Oedipus / Hamlet) as the nuclear complex of every neurosis.
- Fixation (Fixierung) and regression (Regression).

### Fifth Lecture: Transference, Sublimation & Neurosis as Flight
- Flight into sickness (Flucht in die Krankheit) as surrogate satisfaction.
- Transference (Übertragung): Patient projects unconscious affectionate and hostile feelings onto the physician. The physician serves as a catalytic ferment.
- Sublimation (Sublimierung): Transforming erotic impulse energy into higher, culturally valuable goals.
